## Sentinel defence drone

- Original title: **Sci-Fi Flying Drone Robot | Futuristic Drone**
- Creator: **polyNeast**
- Sketchfab asset ID: `4b3f87adb88b4a2fb8037d71e5138391`
- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/4b3f87adb88b4a2fb8037d71e5138391
- License shown by source: **Creative Commons Attribution**
- Changes: removed animation-driven transform faults, centered both rotor discs in their illuminated guards, mirrored the detached left weapon back to its hardpoint, normalized to a 5.2 m span, exported as a static 256k-polygon hero asset, and applied lossless-geometry Meshopt compression.

## Scanned surface geology

- Assets: **Moon Rock 01** (`moon_rock_01`) and **Coastal Cliff 04** (`coastal_cliff_04`)
- Creator/source: **Poly Haven**, https://polyhaven.com
- License: **CC0**
- Changes: selected web LOD for the moon rock, produced a 130k-polygon silhouette-preserving cliff LOD from the scan, normalized pivots to their ground contact plane, retained 2K PBR maps, recolored per biome, and deployed through GPU instancing.

## Basalt planet orbital mosaic

- Product: **Mars Viking Colorized Global Mosaic (MDIM 2.1)**
- Originator: **NASA Ames**; publisher: **USGS Astrogeology Science Center**
- Source: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_colorized_global_mosaic_232m
- License/status: **Public domain**, no use constraints
- Changes: downsampled from the 1 km/pixel equirectangular ancillary JPEG to a 4096×2048 runtime texture; used as a fictional basalt planet surface base with separate lighting, clouds, and atmosphere.
