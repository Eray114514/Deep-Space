# Planet + HUD target prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: high-fidelity 16:9 in-game runtime screenshot target for a WebGL / Three.js third-person space flight game

Primary request: Create a brand-new game visual target, using the supplied screenshot only as a reference for broad composition, rear third-person spaceship viewpoint, and restrained cyan sci-fi atmosphere. This is not an edit and must not copy the blurry planet rendering. Show how the game should look after improvement.

Scene/backdrop: Low orbit above a colossal believable rocky exoplanet. The planet fills most of the frame but reads unmistakably as a sphere: strong visible curvature across the horizon and surface, an oblique grazing camera angle, a clear day-night terminator, deep space and sparse stars beyond the limb.

Subject: A compact dark metallic spacecraft centered slightly below the middle, viewed from behind, flying toward and over the planet. Ship silhouette remains readable but secondary to the planetary scale.

Planet surface: sharply structured geological depth with mountain chains casting long shadows, overlapping ridges, impact craters of multiple scales with raised rims, branching rift valleys and canyons, eroded plateaus, scattered boulder fields, varied roughness and subtle albedo variation. No flat cloudy blobs. Terrain should gain parallax and relief toward the near side while becoming finer toward the horizon. Add a thin cyan atmospheric scattering rim, soft haze following the curved limb, sparse low ground fog pooled inside valleys, and subtle high-altitude wisps. Show physically convincing sunlight, occlusion, rugged PBR materials and large-scale geographic structure. The terminator should reveal topographic silhouettes and make the curvature undeniable. Convey true near-orbit scale with tiny distant terrain detail and atmospheric depth.

Style/medium: polished realistic real-time game rendering, practical WebGL / Three.js art target, physically based materials, high quality but achievable, not a movie poster and not painterly concept art.

Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape gameplay view. Planet is the dominant focal subject; spacecraft centered slightly low. Preserve unobstructed central flight corridor and keep UI near edges. Slightly wide third-person chase-camera lens.

Lighting/mood: cold directional starlight/sunlight from upper left, long terrain shadows, dark space, restrained cyan atmospheric glow, small amber warning accents, subtle violet-blue engine emission. Serious, precise, exploratory mood.

HUD: refined, minimal, implementable sci-fi HUD with excellent hierarchy. Lower-left compact flight-status cluster with segmented thin-line gauges, speed/altitude numerals and a small ship schematic; clean central targeting reticle; upper-right translucent mission card with one dominant objective line represented by very short pseudo-glyphs and a progress marker; top-center slim heading/attitude scale; discreet lower-right system/resource indicators. Add faint peripheral acceleration streaks, subtle radial edge distortion and a narrow engine-energy bloom to communicate thrust without hiding terrain. Use fine cyan/blue-gray lines, dark translucent panels, sparse amber highlights, cut-corner geometry, micro-grid ticks and disciplined spacing. HUD must never cover the planet's key terrain or dominate the scene.

Text: no readable sentences, no long labels, no Chinese or English paragraphs. Allow only a few large numbers, single letters, tiny pseudo-glyphs and compact abstract symbols.

Constraints: convincing planetary sphere and geological relief are the top priority; clear curvature; physically credible terrain scale; HUD looks shippable and code-feasible; spacecraft readable and centered slightly low; no watermark; no logos; no decorative frame; no split screen.

Avoid: blurry camouflage-like texture, flat pasted texture, smooth featureless sphere, cloudy mush, oversized cyan outline, excessive bloom, lens-flare clutter, dense illegible text, hologram overload, anime UI, cinematic title cards, movie-poster layout, photobash seams, impossible fantasy megastructures.
