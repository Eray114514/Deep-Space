# Cloud algorithm sources

`production-shape.js` adapts the rounded height support and separated
weather/shape remapping from `three-geospatial`,
`packages/clouds/src/shaders/clouds.glsl`, commit
`b012ad06d858fc035d88aacfd73f092f93c994e4`.
The project's weather, coordinates, metre scales and optical transport are
independent. Adoption and validation are recorded in the cloud rework review.
